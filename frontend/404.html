HOW TO PLAY

Learn it in four panels

PICK SIDES

Join the red or blue team in the lobby as the spymaster or as an agent. Each team has one spymaster who sees the key.

GIVE A CLUE

The spymaster sends one word and one number. The number says how many board words it connects. A clue cannot match a word on the board.

GUESS

Agents tap words. Right colour and you keep going, up to the number plus one. Wrong colour ends the turn. A timer can be turned on in the lobby.

AVOID THE ✖

Tap the assassin and your team loses at once. Find all your words first to win. Red is ▲ stripes, blue is ● dots, so colour is never the only clue.
